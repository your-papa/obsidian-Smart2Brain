# Smart City Overview

A smart city uses digital technology, data, and connectivity to improve the efficiency of urban services, enhance quality of life, and reduce environmental impact. It is not a single technology but an integrated approach to urban management.

## Core Layers
- **Sensing** — [[IoT Sensors]] embedded across the city.
- **Connectivity** — networks linking devices and systems.
- **Data** — [[Urban Data Platforms]] aggregating and analyzing information.
- **Action** — automated systems and informed decision-making.

## Key Domains
- Energy and utilities, via the [[Smart Grid]].
- Transport, via [[Smart Mobility]].
- Buildings, via [[Smart Buildings]].
- Public services, via [[Smart Waste Management]] and [[Smart Street Lighting]].

## Goals
- Lower carbon emissions and resource use.
- Faster, more responsive public services.
- Greater citizen participation through [[Citizen Engagement]].
