# Hydropower

Hydropower generates electricity from the gravitational energy of falling or flowing water. It is the world's largest source of renewable electricity and provides valuable dispatchable, baseload power.

## Types
- **Reservoir (impoundment)** — large dams storing water for on-demand generation.
- **Run-of-river** — uses natural flow with minimal storage, lower environmental impact.
- **Pumped storage** — a form of [[Energy Storage]] that pumps water uphill during surplus and releases it during peak demand.

## Strengths
- Reliable, dispatchable, and long-lived (50–100 years).
- Provides grid stability and black-start capability.
- Complements variable sources like [[Wind Power]] and [[Solar Photovoltaics]].

## Challenges
- Ecosystem disruption and displacement of communities.
- Methane emissions from reservoirs in tropical regions.
- Vulnerability to drought under climate change.
