# Bioenergy

Bioenergy derives energy from organic matter — crops, forestry residues, agricultural waste, and organic municipal waste. It can produce electricity, heat, and liquid fuels, and is the only renewable source that directly provides drop-in transport fuels.

## Forms
- **Biomass combustion** — direct burning for heat and power.
- **Biogas** — anaerobic digestion of waste into methane.
- **Biofuels** — ethanol and biodiesel for transport.

## Connection to Cities
Bioenergy links directly to [[Smart Waste Management]]: organic waste collected in cities can be converted into biogas and compost, closing the urban resource loop.

## Controversies
- Competition with food crops for land.
- Carbon accounting is debated (is it truly carbon-neutral?).
- Air-quality impacts from combustion.
