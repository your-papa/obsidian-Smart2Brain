# Smart Mobility

Smart mobility uses data and connectivity to make transportation more efficient, accessible, and sustainable. It encompasses everything from real-time transit information to autonomous vehicles.

## Components
- **Mobility-as-a-Service (MaaS)** — integrated apps combining transit, bikes, scooters, and ride-hailing.
- **Intelligent traffic management** — adaptive signals and congestion pricing.
- **Electric vehicles** — charging infrastructure tied to the [[Smart Grid]].
- **Autonomous and connected vehicles** — communicating with infrastructure via [[IoT Sensors]].

## Benefits
- Reduced congestion and travel time.
- Lower emissions, especially when powered by [[Renewable Energy Policy]]-driven clean electricity.
- Better access for underserved communities.

## Challenges
- Data privacy and equitable access.
- Coordination across public and private operators.
- Infrastructure investment for charging and connectivity.
