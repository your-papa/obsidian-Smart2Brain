# IoT Sensors

Internet of Things (IoT) sensors are the sensory nervous system of a smart city — small, networked devices that measure physical conditions and transmit data for analysis and action.

## Common Sensor Types
- **Environmental** — air quality, temperature, humidity, noise.
- **Traffic** — vehicle counts, speed, occupancy.
- **Utility** — smart meters for water, gas, and electricity.
- **Infrastructure** — structural health, vibration, water-leak detection.

## Applications
- Real-time air-quality monitoring to guide public-health responses.
- Smart parking detection feeding [[Smart Mobility]].
- Grid telemetry enabling the [[Smart Grid]].
- Predictive maintenance of bridges and roads.

## Challenges
- Power and connectivity for large-scale deployments.
- Data privacy and security.
- Interoperability across vendors and standards.
