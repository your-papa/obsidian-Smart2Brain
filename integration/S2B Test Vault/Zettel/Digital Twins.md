# Digital Twins

A digital twin is a virtual, real-time replica of a physical asset, system, or entire city. It integrates live data from [[IoT Sensors]] with 3D models and simulation to test scenarios before acting in the real world.

## Applications
- **Urban planning** — simulate traffic, energy, and flood impacts of new developments.
- **Infrastructure management** — monitor bridges, tunnels, and utilities.
- **Energy optimization** — model building and district performance, linking to [[Smart Buildings]] and the [[Smart Grid]].
- **Emergency response** — rehearse evacuation and disaster scenarios.

## Benefits
- Lower-risk, evidence-based decision-making.
- Predictive maintenance and reduced downtime.
- Cross-departmental collaboration on a shared model.

## Challenges
- High cost and complexity of building accurate models.
- Data quality and real-time synchronization.
- Governance of who controls the twin.
