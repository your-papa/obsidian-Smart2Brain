# Renewable Energy Policy

Policy is the primary driver of renewable energy deployment. Market forces alone have historically favored incumbent fossil fuels, so governments use a mix of instruments to accelerate the transition.

## Policy Instruments
- **Feed-in tariffs and power purchase agreements** — guaranteed prices for renewable producers.
- **Renewable portfolio standards** — mandates for a share of clean electricity.
- **Tax credits and subsidies** — reduce upfront capital costs.
- **Carbon pricing** — internalizes the cost of emissions.
- **Net metering** — compensates rooftop [[Solar Photovoltaics]] owners.

## Urban Policy
Cities enact their own measures — building codes requiring solar-ready roofs, EV-charging mandates, and district-energy plans — which overlap with [[Smart City Governance]].

## Challenges
- Policy uncertainty deters long-term investment.
- Balancing affordability with decarbonization goals.
- Grid access and permitting bottlenecks.
