# Green Hydrogen

Green hydrogen is produced by electrolyzing water using renewable electricity, splitting H₂O into hydrogen and oxygen with no carbon emissions. It is a versatile energy carrier that can decarbonize sectors electricity cannot easily reach.

## Production
- **Electrolysis** powered by surplus [[Solar Photovoltaics]] and [[Wind Power]].
- Coupled with [[Energy Storage]] to absorb excess renewable generation.

## Applications
- **Heavy industry** — steel, cement, and chemicals.
- **Long-haul transport** — shipping, aviation, and heavy trucking.
- **Seasonal storage** — storing renewable energy for months.

## Challenges
- High cost compared to fossil-derived hydrogen.
- Significant energy losses in the round-trip conversion.
- Requires new transport and storage infrastructure.
