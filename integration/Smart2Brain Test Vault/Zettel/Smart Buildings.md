# Smart Buildings

Smart buildings use automation, sensors, and data to optimize energy use, comfort, and operations. Buildings account for a large share of urban energy consumption, making them a critical target for efficiency.

## Key Systems
- **Building management systems (BMS)** — central control of HVAC, lighting, and security.
- **Smart lighting** — occupancy and daylight sensors.
- **Demand response** — buildings that reduce load when the [[Smart Grid]] signals stress.
- **On-site generation** — rooftop [[Solar Photovoltaics]] and [[Energy Storage]].

## Benefits
- Significant energy and cost savings.
- Improved occupant comfort and productivity.
- Grid services through flexible demand.

## Challenges
- Retrofitting legacy buildings is costly.
- Cybersecurity of connected building systems.
- Interoperability between subsystems.
