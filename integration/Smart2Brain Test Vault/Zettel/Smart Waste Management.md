# Smart Waste Management

Smart waste management uses sensors and data to optimize the collection, sorting, and processing of urban waste, reducing costs and environmental impact.

## Technologies
- **Fill-level sensors** in bins that trigger collection only when full.
- **Route optimization** for collection fleets.
- **Smart sorting** using computer vision and robotics.
- **Waste-to-energy** — converting organic waste to biogas, linking to [[Bioenergy]].

## Benefits
- Fewer collection trips and lower emissions.
- Reduced overflow and cleaner streets.
- Higher recycling and diversion rates.

## Circular Economy
Smart waste systems are a cornerstone of the circular economy, feeding recovered materials back into production and organic waste into energy and compost.
