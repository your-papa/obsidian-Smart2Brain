# Smart Street Lighting

Smart street lighting replaces conventional lamps with connected LED fixtures that can be dimmed, scheduled, and monitored remotely. It is often the first smart-city project a municipality deploys because of its rapid payback.

## Capabilities
- **Adaptive dimming** — brighten on motion or traffic, dim when empty.
- **Remote monitoring** — instant fault detection and maintenance dispatch.
- **Sensor hosting** — poles serve as mounting points for [[IoT Sensors]] (cameras, air quality, Wi-Fi).

## Benefits
- Energy savings of 50–70% over legacy lighting.
- Reduced maintenance costs.
- A ready-made network backbone for other smart-city services.

## Connection to the Grid
Smart lighting is a demand-response resource for the [[Smart Grid]] and complements efficiency measures in [[Smart Buildings]].
