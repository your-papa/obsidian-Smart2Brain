# Citizen Engagement

Citizen engagement ensures that smart-city initiatives serve the people who live in them, not just the technology vendors. It turns residents from passive recipients into active participants.

## Methods
- **Participatory budgeting** — residents vote on how to allocate funds.
- **Civic apps** — reporting potholes, broken lights, and hazards.
- **Open data portals** — transparency via [[Urban Data Platforms]].
- **Co-design workshops** — involving communities in planning.

## Why It Matters
- Builds trust and legitimacy for smart-city projects.
- Surfaces needs that top-down planning misses.
- Reduces the risk of "tech for tech's sake" failures.

## Challenges
- Digital divide — ensuring all residents can participate.
- Sustaining engagement beyond one-off consultations.
- Balancing expert input with public opinion.
