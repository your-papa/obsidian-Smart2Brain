# Smart City Governance

Smart city governance is the framework of policies, institutions, and processes that guide how technology is deployed and managed in a city. Good governance determines whether smart-city investments deliver public value.

## Key Elements
- **Strategy and vision** — a clear, citizen-centered roadmap.
- **Data governance** — rules for privacy, security, and sharing via [[Urban Data Platforms]].
- **Procurement** — avoiding vendor lock-in and ensuring interoperability.
- **Cross-sector collaboration** — public, private, and academic partnerships.
- **Accountability** — transparent metrics and oversight.

## Link to Energy
Urban governance increasingly coordinates with energy policy, aligning city climate goals with [[Renewable Energy Policy]] and the [[Smart Grid]].

## Challenges
- Fragmented authority across agencies.
- Balancing innovation with privacy and equity.
- Long-term funding and political continuity.
