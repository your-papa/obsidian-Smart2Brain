# Grid Integration Challenges

Integrating high shares of variable renewable energy into the grid presents technical, economic, and regulatory challenges that must be solved for the transition to succeed.

## Key Challenges
- **Variability and uncertainty** — output from [[Solar Photovoltaics]] and [[Wind Power]] fluctuates with weather.
- **The duck curve** — steep evening ramps as solar fades and demand peaks.
- **Transmission constraints** — best renewable resources are often far from load centers.
- **Inertia loss** — conventional plants provide grid inertia that inverters must now emulate.
- **Curtailment** — wasted generation when supply exceeds demand or transmission capacity.

## Solutions
- [[Energy Storage]] to time-shift generation.
- [[Smart Grid]] automation and demand response.
- Improved forecasting and regional market integration.
- [[Green Hydrogen]] to absorb surplus power.
