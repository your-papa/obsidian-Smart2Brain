# Energy Storage

Energy storage decouples electricity generation from consumption, making it the linchpin of a high-renewable grid. It smooths the variability of [[Solar Photovoltaics]] and [[Wind Power]] and provides fast-response grid services.

## Storage Technologies
- **Lithium-ion batteries** — dominant for short-duration (1–4 hour) storage and behind-the-meter use.
- **Pumped hydro** — the largest source of grid-scale storage globally.
- **Flow batteries** — scalable for longer durations.
- **Compressed air and thermal storage** — emerging for long-duration needs.
- **Green hydrogen** — seasonal-scale storage via [[Green Hydrogen]].

## Applications
- Frequency regulation and grid stabilization.
- Peak shaving and load shifting.
- Backup power for [[Smart Buildings]] and microgrids.

## Challenges
- Cost and supply-chain constraints for critical minerals.
- Degradation and recycling of batteries.
- Long-duration storage remains expensive.
