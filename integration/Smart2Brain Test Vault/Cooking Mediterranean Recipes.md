# Cooking Mediterranean Recipes

#cooking #food #recipes

The Mediterranean diet emphasizes fresh vegetables, olive oil, whole grains, and lean proteins. These recipes showcase the vibrant flavors of the region.

## Greek Salad (Horiatiki)

A classic summer dish combining ripe tomatoes, cucumbers, red onion, Kalamata olives, and feta cheese. Dress with extra virgin olive oil, dried oregano, and a splash of red wine vinegar. Never add lettuce to a traditional horiatiki.

## Shakshuka

Poached eggs in a spiced tomato sauce with peppers and onions. Season generously with cumin, paprika, and cayenne. Serve with crusty bread for dipping. This North African dish has become a breakfast staple across the Mediterranean.

## Lemon Herb Grilled Fish

Score whole sea bass or branzino, stuff with lemon slices and fresh herbs (thyme, rosemary, parsley), then grill over medium-high heat. The peculiar combination of charred skin and tender, flaky flesh creates an unforgettable flavor.

## Hummus from Scratch

Blend cooked chickpeas with tahini, lemon juice, garlic, and ice water for the smoothest texture. The secret is removing the chickpea skins and using high-quality tahini. Garnish with olive oil, paprika, and whole chickpeas.
